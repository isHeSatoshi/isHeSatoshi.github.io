  1. Anthropochrony

    Post-training · 2026

  2. RagaYourWay

    Audio ML · Live
    Try live ↗

    Upload a Carnatic notation sheet (Roman text, Unicode Kannada, or a scanned songbook PDF) and hear it played on Yamaha C5 grand piano, mridangam, and tambura. Built around CoarticulatedGamakaNet trained on 11,552 vocal svaras.

    56.7% lower pitch-curve error vs 12-TET piano · 102 ragas · native Kannada OCR and live web player

  3. SmallJev

    Open-weights LLM · 2026
    GitHub ↗

    An open 2.5B variant of TypeSafe's Jev with span-pooled semantic scoring. It scores every allowed option in one forward pass without generating tokens, returning calibrated probabilities from an RTX 4060 down to phones.

    2.5B Apache-2.0 · 65.53/100 on public JevBench v1.2 · published on the official leaderboard

  4. localbanana
