I'm an AI research engineer. I work on models, agents, evals, ML harnesses and inference: improving pretrained autonomous-driving models with reinforcement learning, shipping production RAG, and distilling models down until they run on a consumer GPU.

Most of my time goes into measuring things rather than guessing: how attention and quantization actually behave at runtime, whether an eval set is really holding out what it claims, and where the architecture breaks before production finds it.
