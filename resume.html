Perceiving Only When the World Changes: A Guaranteed Sampling Law for Model-Agnostic Video Perception

Universal Reducer · 2026

  • A deterministic state machine in front of any object detector: idle every 0.4 s, fire on straddle, hot-cap every 0.1 s while a task entity is actively crossing the band midline.
  • Proven recall guarantee (Lemmas 1–3): any midline crossing the detector would see at full rate is reported with bounded timestamp error ≤ 0.4 s + 0.5/fps. Property tests pin the boundary cases.
  • Persistent world memory carries identity and motion across idle gaps — no upstream tracker required.
  • On 8 real aerial clips (YOLOv8s @ 640, VisDrone + VIRAT, RTX 4060 Ti): 25–42% of windows sampled, up to 78.7% wall-clock reduction; GT eval covers every midline crossing at a 0.3 s cadence (60/60).
  • Trained-free, model-agnostic, tracker-free — a patent and literature search found no prior art teaching the combination.
  • Also covers the broader Persistent Perception Computing line of work: contextual & event-aware video AI, task-oriented perception orchestration, evidence & audit workflows, plug-and-play perception backends.
