Research

Perceiving Only When the World Changes: A Guaranteed Sampling Law for Model-Agnostic Video Perception

Universal Reducer · 2026

  • A deterministic state machine goes in front of any object detector: idle every 0.4 s, fire on straddle, hot-cap every 0.1 s while a task entity is crossing the band midline.
  • Recall is bounded by Lemmas 1–3. Any midline crossing the detector would have seen at full rate gets reported with timestamp error ≤ 0.4 s + 0.5/fps. Property tests cover the boundary cases.
  • World memory holds identity and motion across the idle gaps, so nothing upstream has to track anything.
  • On 8 real aerial clips (YOLOv8s @ 640, VisDrone + VIRAT, RTX 4060 Ti): 25–42% of windows sampled, up to 78.7% less wall-clock. The GT eval covers every midline crossing at a 0.3 s cadence, 60 of 60.
  • Training-free, model-agnostic, tracker-free. A patent and literature search turned up no prior art on that combination.
